## When does a Stop hook run?
### Answer:
A Stop hook runs when Claude finishes responding or stops its current operation. It can execute a script or perform checks before the session ends.

## What are zero-shot, one-shot, and few-shot prompting?
### Answer:
- Zero-shot prompting: Asking AI to perform a task without giving any examples.
- One-shot prompting: Giving AI one example to show the expected output.
- Few-shot prompting: Giving AI a more than one examples to help it understand the desired output.

## What is the 4D framework?
### Answer:
The 4D Framework is a method for working effectively with AI:
- Delegation: Decide which tasks to give to AI.
- Description: Clearly explain the task and expected outcome.
- Discernment: Evaluate and verify AI-generated results.
- Diligence: Use AI responsibly and check the final output.

## What is the difference between Skills and Hooks?
### Answer:
- Skills: Provide Claude with reusable instructions and knowledge for performing specific tasks.
- Hooks: Automatically execute commands or scripts when specific events occur in Claude Code.

## What is the difference between a direct API and a managed agent?
### Answer:
- Direct API: You directly call an AI model and manage the workflow, tools, and conversation logic yourself.
- Managed Agent: A platform manages the agent's workflow, tool usage, and execution, reducing the amount of code you need to write.
